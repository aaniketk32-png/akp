boAt

Loud · Gen Z · Hinglish

Talks like your funniest friend on the group chat. Short lines, big energy, a little cheeky, never mean.

Campaign · Wedding season

Shaadi season. Survival mode: on.

December means three weddings, forty relatives and the same question at every table: "Beta, tumhari shaadi kab?"

The idea: boAt earbuds as your polite escape. Smile, nod, and let the music do the talking.

Social copy

  • Instagram postRishtedaar: "Beta, kab?"
    You, with earbuds in: 🎶🙂
  • Reel captionPlan for the sangeet: dance for 10 minutes, then disappear into your playlist for 3 hours. Tag your shaadi survival partner 👇
  • Story pollYour wedding mood: 🕺 Dance floor or 🎧 Corner with snacks?

15-second reel script

TimeOn screenAudio / text
0–3sAunty walks towards him across the wedding lawn.Text: "Incoming: 'Beta, shaadi kab?'"
3–9sHe slips in his boAt earbuds and smiles. Her mouth moves, we hear only the beat.Music drops in, loud and happy.
9–13sShe gives up, pats his cheek and walks away. He grooves alone.Text: "Mission complete."
13–15sLogo on the earbud case."boAt. Shaadi season survival kit."

Why it works: every young Indian knows this moment. The product is the punchline, so the ad sells without feeling like an ad.

Bru

Warm · Homely · Conversational

Sounds like home. Gentle, familiar, a little nostalgic. Talks about small moments, not big claims.

Campaign · Winter mornings

The 6 AM club.

In winter, the best part of the day happens before anyone else wakes up: the quiet kitchen, the cold floor, the first warm cup.

The idea: celebrate the early risers of every home, the mums, dads and night-shift workers, with Bru as their first moment for themselves.

Social copy

  • Instagram postThe house is asleep. The kettle isn't. Welcome to the 6 AM club ☕
  • CarouselSlide 1: Who's in the 6 AM club?
    Slide 2: The mum who packs three tiffins.
    Slide 3: The dad who reads the paper twice.
    Slide 4: You, with your first cup. Members only.
  • Comment promptWho's the first one awake in your house? Tag them and give them their membership 👇

20-second film script

TimeOn screenVoiceover
0–5sDark kitchen, foggy window, a mother switches on the light."Every home has one person who wakes up first."
5–13sShe makes a cup of Bru, holds it with both hands, steam rising."Before the tiffins, the alarms and the 'where are my socks'…"
13–18sShe takes the first sip by the window. A small smile."…there's one cup that's just hers."
18–20sPack shot."Bru. For the 6 AM club."

Why it works: it builds loyalty through a feeling people already have about their mornings. The campaign can run all winter and invite real stories from the audience.

Kimirica

Calm · Luxurious · Sensory

Speaks softly and slowly. Uses texture, scent and time. Never shouts, never discounts in the headline.

Campaign · Gifting season

Gift someone a pause.

December gifts are usually things. The idea: a Kimirica gift is time, a long bath or a quiet evening, for the person who never takes one.

Every piece of copy talks about the moment, then the product.

Social copy

  • Instagram postFor the friend who replies to emails at midnight: twenty quiet minutes, wrapped.
  • Gift guide copyFor her: a slow evening. For him: a long shower he didn't rush. For you: the same. You've earned it.
  • Packaging card lineOpen this when the world gets loud.

Taglines to test

  • Gift someone a pause.
  • Slow is the new luxury.
  • Twenty minutes, just for you.

Why it works: luxury buyers don't want a sale, they want a feeling. "A pause" turns a bath product into a thoughtful gift, and it works in store, online and on the box.
