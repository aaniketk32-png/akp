Six samples · Two brands, two voices

iYURA sounds like a ritual. A. Modernica sounds like a lab notebook.

Three samples for each brand, covering ads, a video script, an email, sales copy, names and taglines. Every line follows a few old rules: lead with facts, put the brand in the headline, give one big idea per brand, and never claim what the product page can't back up.

iYURABig idea: the ritual is the routine. One product, one minute, the Ayurvedic way.

Hooks to test

Oil spreads further and sinks in faster on damp skin. So the best time to use iYURA Balaayah is two minutes after your shower, while your skin is still warm.

Massage in a few drops. It's made with black gram, a staple of Ayurvedic body care, and has 3,341 reviews. Try it for 30 days, or get your money back.

Sample 5 · Long-form sales copy · Product page

A. Modernica Rufolia: five ingredients for the thinnest skin on your face

A fragrance-free eye cream that pairs an Ayurvedic root with modern skincare. 15 g. $28.

The skin around your eyes is the thinnest on your face, so it shows a late night first. Rufolia was made for that one small area, with herbs and actives in the same tube.

What's inside, in one line each

  • Manjistha: an Ayurvedic root for a brighter, more even look
  • Niacinamide (B3): helps reduce the look of dullness
  • Hyaluronic acid: hydration for a smoother, plumper look
  • Sacred lotus and aloe: calm and moisturise

How to use

Three pea-sized dots: under the eye, at the crow's feet, on the lid. Pat in a C-shape. Morning and night.

Who it's for

All skin types. Fragrance-free and dermatologically tested.

The promise

Use it for 30 days. If you're not happy, send it back, even opened, for a full refund.

Try Rufolia for 30 days · $28

Why it works: it follows the classic order of problem, how it works, proof and offer. Each ingredient gets one plain line, so a doubtful reader understands the formula in ten seconds.

Sample 6 · Naming, taglines and an audio sting

A simpler name for ads

"Periorbital Eyemulsion" is accurate, but hard to say. In ads I'd call it "The 3-Dot Eye Cream". It describes exactly how you use it, and people remember a habit faster than a word.

Other options

Rufolia Wake-Up Cream · Rufolia Eye Reset

Taglines

  • Ayurveda, measured.
  • Herbs and actives, in the same tube.
  • Look like you slept.

5-second audio sting

"Dot. Dot. Dot. Done. Rufolia."

Why it works: the nickname, the tagline and the sting all repeat one idea, so every ad adds to the same brand picture.

On claims: every line stays cosmetic ("brighter-looking", "look of dullness") and uses only facts from the brands' public pages: ingredients, sizes, prices, review counts, packaging art and the 30-day guarantee. Final copy would go through your review team.
