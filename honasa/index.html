02 · My idea

One problem, one idea

The problem I’d solve: a first-time buyer who doesn’t come back to the brand’s own site.

The big idea

Your routine lives here.

Other apps sell you a product. The brand’s own site remembers your skin, your routine and when you’ll run out. That’s a reason to come back that no marketplace can copy.

Today they think“I’ll buy it wherever it’s fastest.”
→
We want them to think“The brand’s site knows my routine. I reorder there.”

03 · What I’ll do as your content strategist

Five things I’ll own

  1. 01

    Know the buyer

    Dig into reviews, comments and data to find why first-time buyers leave, and what would bring them back.

  2. 02

    Say one clear thing

    Every brand gets one message per audience. For D2C: “Your routine lives here.”

  3. 03

    Content for every stage

    Content cohorts that take someone from first order to second order to regular customer.

  4. 04

    Scripts and ads that sell

    Short-form scripts, performance ads for Meta and Google, and WhatsApp and email copy, written with designers and editors.

  5. 05

    Track, learn, scale

    Watch repeat rate, site orders and RoAS every week. Put more money behind what works, and cut what doesn’t.

04 · Samples

Content cohorts

Four series, each with one job. I’ve used Mamaearth as the example, since it’s the biggest brand and has its own site.

Find Your Routine

First order

A 60-second skin quiz on the site, promoted with reels and Story polls.

  • “Oily, dry or confused? Take the quiz.”
  • Creators take the quiz live and react to their result

How To Use It Right

Days 1–7

Short how-to content so the first product actually works, and people see results.

  • “How much face wash is enough?”
  • “Morning vs night: what goes first?”

Real Results, Real People

Days 7–21

Customer stories and wear tests that build trust with proof instead of hype.

  • 30-day diary from real buyers
  • Reviews turned into carousel posts

Running Low?

Days 21–30 · reorder

Refill reminders and “complete your routine” ideas, with a one-tap reorder on the brand’s site.

  • WhatsApp reminder before the bottle runs out
  • “You loved the face wash. Meet its best friend.”
