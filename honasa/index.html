02 · Brief 1 · Mamaearth

Smog season: Routine by AQI

Ask anyone in Delhi this week what their cotton pad looks like after a day out. That’s where this brief started. People in NCR come home with dull, gritty skin and flat hair, and they’re tired of brands using fear to sell in smog season. So this plan helps first and sells second.

1Where the growth comes from

Category penetration×Brand penetration×Frequency×Amount per use=Brand size
Category

Not the gap

Most city 20–35s already use a face wash. We don’t need to sell the habit.

Brand penetration

Win the smog-season switchers

People pick up whatever promises “pollution” on the shelf. We want them to pick Mamaearth this winter.

Frequency

A second cleanse at night

On bad AQI days, add one proper cleanse at night. More use means the bottle runs out sooner, and the reorder comes sooner.

2Who we’re talking to

Daily commutersOut 8–10 hours, metro, cab, bike. Feel the grime every evening.Our target
Work from homeLess exposure, less urgency.
Parents of young kidsCare a lot, but it’s a health topic. Too sensitive to sell to.

3Positioning

For NCR’s 20–35s who come home every day with smog on their skin, Mamaearth is the everyday skincare brand that actually shows up for Delhi, with a simple routine for every AQI level.

Why believe it: it already put anti-smog guns on Gurugram’s roads last December, its Charcoal Face Wash is made for oil control and pollution, and it tells you to wear a mask too.

4The idea

The idea

Routine by AQI.

When the air changes, your routine changes. Small, simple tips for each AQI level, made with Delhi creators. Mamaearth plays the helping hand.

Today they think“Smog is about my lungs. My skincare can wait.”
→
We want them to think“On bad air days I clean up properly at night. Mamaearth actually shows up for Delhi.”

“I want to convince 20–35s in NCR that a few small changes on smoggy days make a visible difference, and that Mamaearth is the brand that’s here for Delhi.”

Useful firstTips lead. Products follow.
Why nowAQI above 450, and Mamaearth turns 10 in December.
The ruleNo fear, no health claims. A face wash never replaces a mask.

What we post, by AQI level

AQI 100–200Normal days. Cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen. Hazy skies still let UV through.
AQI 200–300Add a proper night cleanse with the Charcoal Face Wash. Wash hair a little more often.
AQI 300–400Cleanse twice at night, use a richer moisturiser, and wash your scalp after long days out.
AQI 400+Stay in when you can, mask on. Short, gentle routine. We pause sales posts and share tips only.

03 · Brief 2 · BBlunt

Salon Secret relaunch: #ShiningBaby is back

Picture Neha the night before her cousin’s sangeet: a box of hair colour in one hand, a YouTube tutorial paused on her phone. That pause is the moment BBlunt has to win. The relaunched Salon Secret High Shine hair colour is already pulling BBlunt’s growth. Its own lab test found 6x better colour retention after 3 washes and up to 92% less breakage than another product on the market (tested on hair samples). And in 2017, its #ShiningBaby challenge had 37 celebrities doing Bollywood dialogues. Time to bring the shine back.

1Where the growth comes from

Category penetration×Brand penetration×Frequency×Amount per use=Brand size
Category penetration

Bring in first-timers

Lots of people in their 20s and 30s have never coloured at home. That’s the biggest group to win.

Brand penetration

Switchers come next

Win people from mass hair colour brands once the shine story is known.

Frequency

The root touch-up

Roots show again in 4–6 weeks. A reminder with the exact shade turns one box into a habit.

2Who we’re talking to

First-timers, 22–40Curious about colour, or spotting first greys. Scared of getting it wrong.Our target
Salon regularsWant cheaper touch-ups between salon visits.
Grey coverage, 45+Already loyal to a brand. Harder to move.

04 · How I’d run it

Same playbook for both briefs

Ideas are the easy part. This is how I’d make them pay off, week after week.

Test

3 hooks, one offer

Humour, a question, a creator. Same budget. Cut the weakest once there’s enough data, scale the winner, refresh every 2–3 weeks.

Watch

Past the clicks

Hook rate and CTR for the creative. Cost per purchase and RoAS for the money. Reorders on the brand’s site to see if people come back.

Team

A simple weekly rhythm

Monday: briefs out. Wednesday: first cuts reviewed with design and video. Friday: last week’s numbers decide next week’s work.

Safe

ASCI from draft one

Every claim checked against its source, lab results worded as tested, every creator post disclosed.
