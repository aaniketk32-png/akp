01 · Brand objective

Start with the objective

Aqualogica’s own campaign already says it well: for years, sun protection has felt like a hassle. Plenty of young people own a sunscreen. The problem is how often they use it.

Brand objective

Grow how often people use it

Turn occasional users into daily users by building new everyday occasions, not just chasing new buyers.

Core audience

The Weekend Sunscreeners

18–26, college or first job, metro India. They own a sunscreen but only use it for trips, beach plans and long days out.

Market×Penetration×Frequency×Amount per use=Brand size

Of the four levers, I’d pull two. Frequency: more days of use. Amount per use: applying enough for real protection, with the “two-finger rule” built into the creative. Both grow sales from people who already know the brand.

The big idea

The sun doesn’t check your plans. Your sunscreen shouldn’t either.

It builds on Aqualogica’s existing Mr. Sun character from Feels So Light, Shields So Right. Instead of a one-off film, Mr. Sun becomes a recurring social character who finds you on every “normal” day: the window seat, the balcony chai, the auto ride.

EssenceSun protection is an everyday thing, not a trip thing.
Resolves the barrier“I’m home today” and “it feels heavy”.
MemorableA character people can recognise and tag their friends in.

Why this segment

Big and winnable

A large group, already in the category and already aware of Aqualogica, with a barrier that the product’s texture can actually solve.

Positioning

The sunscreen you forget you’re wearing

For young Indians who skip sunscreen on “normal” days, Aqualogica is the SPF that feels light enough to wear every day.

03 · Communication challenge

From “trip only” to “every day”

Today

They think: “Sunscreen is for outdoor plans.”

They do: Apply it on trip days, skip it the rest of the week.

→

Where we want them

They think: “It’s so light, I don’t even notice it.”

They do: Put it on every morning, like brushing their teeth.

“I want to convince Weekend Sunscreeners that Aqualogica feels so light, wearing it every day is easier than skipping it.”

05 · Content cohorts

Five series, one funnel

Each series has one job and one stage of the journey, so we always know what a post is for and how to judge it.

Mr. Sun Finds You

Awareness

A comedy series in which Mr. Sun shows up on everyday “indoor” days.

  • Mr. Sun at the window seat in the office
  • Mr. Sun at your cousin’s day wedding
  • Mr. Sun on the metro platform

15-Second Skin Questions

Consideration

Quick answers to the questions people actually Google, reviewed by the brand’s skin experts.

  • Do I need sunscreen if I’m home all day?
  • How much is enough for the face?
  • Can I reapply over makeup?

Get Ready, For Real

Consideration → First try

Creators and customers showing real skin and real routines, with no filters on the texture shot.

  • 30-second morning routine
  • Midday reapply check in Delhi heat
  • Wear test from 9 AM to 6 PM

Which Plus Are You?

Conversion

An Instagram quiz and Story polls that match a skin concern to the right range, such as Radiance+, Detan+ or Barrier+, with a shop link at the end.

  • “Post-trip tan or everyday dullness?”
  • Carousel: one concern, one product, one reason

The 1 PM Reapply Club

Repeat → Loyalty

WhatsApp and Story reminders at reapply time, with streaks and community shout-outs that keep the habit going.

  • “It’s 1 PM. Mr. Sun is at his strongest. You know what to do.”
  • Monthly “30-day streak” feature for followers

08 · Media plan

Paid, owned, earned

Each type of media does a different job, and each one feeds the next.

Paid

Meta and Google

Reels ads and creator partnership ads for reach. Search ads on high-intent terms like “sunscreen for oily skin” or “lightweight sunscreen”, where people are ready to buy.

Owned

Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, website

The five series, the “Which Plus Are You?” quiz on the site, and 1 PM reapply reminders for people who opted in.

Earned

Creators and customers

A #MrSunFoundMe challenge: people duet the reel with the place Mr. Sun found them. The best entries get reposted, and that builds advocacy.

09 · Test, learn, scale

How I’d judge what works

Testing plan

  1. Run three hooks (humour, question, creator) against the same offer and budget.
  2. Cut the weakest after enough spend to judge, based on hook rate and click-through.
  3. Put more budget behind the winner, and turn top organic reels into ads with the creators.
  4. Refresh creative every 2–3 weeks, before people get tired of it.

What I’d measure at each stage

StageSignal
Awareness3-second views, reach, shares
ConsiderationSaves, comments, quiz completions
ConversionCTR, add to cart, cost per purchase, RoAS
RepeatRepeat purchase when the first tube runs out
