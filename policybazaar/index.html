It started with a reel about plot twists.

"One simple rule can change how you think about your money"

31 Oct · 40.2K views · 11 likes

The problem
A great topic told as a definition. It reached 40,000 people and kept almost none of them.
What I did
"Your money has a doubling date." A 7-slide carousel with real numbers at 2% and 6%, then inflation flipped the other way.
Reason and logic
"Your money" makes it personal, and a number to work out gives people something to comment. Math and checklists get saved. Measure: saves and shares per 1,000 views
See all 7 slides

My version: a 7-slide carousel

  1. Your money has a doubling date. Most people never check it.
  2. The Rule of 72: divide 72 by your yearly return. That's roughly how many years your money takes to double.
  3. Savings growing at 2% a year? 72 ÷ 2 = 36 years.
  4. Growing at 6% a year? 72 ÷ 6 = 12 years.
  5. Now flip it. Prices rising 3% a year? In about 24 years, the same money buys half as much.
  6. Saving is step one. Knowing how fast it grows, and keeping it protected, is step two.
  7. Save this. Do the math on your own savings. Tell us your doubling date 👇

Caption: We did the math so you don't have to. What's your doubling date? Drop your number below. (General information, not financial advice. The rates are examples.)

My version: "Your AECB score is basically your gym routine"

  1. Your AECB score is basically your gym routine. Hear us out.
  2. Paying on time = showing up. Miss sessions and the progress goes.
  3. Maxing out your card = skipping leg day. A common rule of thumb is to keep usage under 30% of your limit.
  4. Applying for five cards at once = crash dieting. Lots of activity, worse results.
  5. Checking your score = stepping on the scale. It doesn't hurt the score, and you know where you stand.
  6. Scores in the UAE run from 300 to 900. Check yours free on the Paisabazaar.ae app.
  7. Save this for your next "new year, new me". Which habit are you skipping? 👇

Count what people do, not what they see

Main number

Engagement per 1,000 views

Likes, comments, shares and saves divided by views. A promoted reel with 40K views and 11 likes scores close to zero, and that's the honest picture.

Comments

Fix the hook before the budget
