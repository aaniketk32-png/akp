"Your trip can have a lot of plot twists ✈️"

Posted 15 Oct · 495 views · 4 likes · 0 comments

Why it didn't land

  • It promised plot twists and never showed one.
  • The price arrived in the second line, so it read as an ad before it became a story.
  • Nothing asked the viewer to join in.

My version: "Expat Plot Twists", ep. 1

0–3s

"Ten days in Europe. Everything booked."

Creator at DXB with a suitcase, smiling. On screen: My winter trip, as planned ✈️

3–6s

Record scratch.

On screen: Plot twist #1: flight cancelled.

6–10s

"My bag went to Frankfurt. I went to Rome."

On screen: Plot twist #2: lost baggage.

10–14s

"Day two. Food poisoning. Doctors abroad aren't cheap."

On screen: Plot twist #3: hospital visit.

14–20s

"Best plot twist? I'd bought travel insurance before I flew."

Calm cut, creator relaxed at the hotel. End card: Pack the insurance before the sunscreen. Travel cover from AED 22* on Policybazaar.ae

Caption: Which plot twist has happened to you? 👇 Ours was the bag that went on holiday without us. Travel cover from AED 22* on Policybazaar.ae. *T&Cs apply

Why this works

  • Story first, product as the happy ending. The insurance solves the problem we just watched.
  • Winter travel season makes it timely, and every expat has a travel horror story.
  • The caption asks for stories, so people comment and tag travel buddies.
  • It's a series. Next episodes: the first car fine, the first visa medical, the first summer DEWA bill.

Measure: comments and shares per 1,000 views.

Rework 2 · Expert interview

Fast Track Life: rapid-fire with an insurer's head of bancassurance

Posted 19 Oct · 593 views · 5 likes · 0 comments

Why it didn't land

  • The hook ("What would you ask a life insurance expert…") makes the viewer do the work.
  • The guest's job title leads. Viewers care about their question, not his title.
  • Many questions in one video means no single takeaway to remember or share.

My version: "One Question. 30 Seconds."

0–3s

"If nothing happens to me, isn't term insurance a waste of money?"

The question appears as a comment screenshot, read out by the host. Big on-screen text.

3–25s

The expert answers in plain words, one point only.

Tight frame, subtitles 3–5 words a line, one key phrase highlighted.

25–30s

"Got a question? Comment it. It could be next week's."

End card: One Question. 30 Seconds. · Policybazaar.ae

Next questions, all from real worries: "My company gives me life cover. Is that enough?" · "Can my family back home claim if I'm in Dubai?" · "How much cover do I actually need?" · "What happens to my policy if I leave the UAE?"

Why this works

  • Keeps the best thing they have, real experts, and fixes the packaging.
  • One idea per clip, so each one is easy to finish, save and send.
  • The audience writes the next episode. Comments become content, and the feed starts conversations.
  • One shoot, six reels. The same hour with an expert fills six weeks.

Measure: completion rate, saves, and questions left in comments.

Rework 3 · Money explainer

"One simple rule can change how you think about your money": the Rule of 72

Posted 31 Oct · 40.2K views · 11 likes

Why it didn't land

  • A great topic delivered as a definition.
  • No personal stakes. It explains a rule, but never shows what it means for your money.
  • It reached 40,000 people and kept almost none of them.

My version: a 7-slide carousel

  1. Your money has a doubling date. Most people never check it.
  2. The Rule of 72: divide 72 by your yearly return. That's roughly how many years your money takes to double.
  3. Savings growing at 2% a year? 72 ÷ 2 = 36 years.
  4. Growing at 6% a year? 72 ÷ 6 = 12 years.
  5. Now flip it. Prices rising 3% a year? In about 24 years, the same money buys half as much.
  6. Saving is step one. Knowing how fast it grows, and keeping it protected, is step two.
  7. Save this. Do the math on your own savings. Tell us your doubling date 👇

Caption: We did the math so you don't have to. What's your doubling date? Drop your number below. (General information, not financial advice. The rates are examples.)

Why this works

  • "Your money" turns a rule into something personal.
  • A number to work out gives people something to comment.
  • Saves are a strong signal, and checklists and math get saved.
  • No product push. It builds the "smart friend" trust that pays off at renewal.

Measure: saves and shares per 1,000 views.

On screen: 2. Network

On screen: 3. Co-pay

Rework 3 · Credit score

"A better credit score doesn't happen overnight"

Posted 12 Nov · 2,692 views · 19 likes

Why it didn't land

  • The right topic, but the tips are ones people have heard before.
  • Nothing makes them memorable.
  • No reason to save or share it.

My version: "Your AECB score is basically your gym routine"

  1. Your AECB score is basically your gym routine. Hear us out.
  2. Paying on time = showing up. Miss sessions and the progress goes.
  3. Maxing out your card = skipping leg day. A common rule of thumb is to keep usage under 30% of your limit.
  4. Applying for five cards at once = crash dieting. Lots of activity, worse results.
  5. Checking your score = stepping on the scale. It doesn't hurt the score, and you know where you stand.
  6. Scores in the UAE run from 300 to 900. Check yours free on the Paisabazaar.ae app.
  7. Save this for your next "new year, new me". Which habit are you skipping? 👇

Why this works

  • An analogy makes dry advice stick.
  • Timely: it lands right after the Dubai Fitness Challenge.
  • Built to be saved, with a soft path to a free score check.

Measure: saves and shares.

10 · How I'd measure it, for both brands

Count what people do, not what they see

Main number

Engagement per 1,000 views

Likes, comments, shares and saves divided by views. A promoted reel with 40K views and 11 likes scores close to zero, and that's the honest picture.

Saves and shares

The signals that grow reach

Explainers should be saved. Skits should be shared. Each post gets one target.

Comments

Questions become content

Every week, the best question in the comments becomes next week's expert clip.

One rule

Fix the hook before the budget

If a promoted reel gets reach but no engagement, rework the first 3 seconds before spending more on it.
